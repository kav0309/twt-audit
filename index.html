Creative Brief Recommendations
Strategy Recommendation

Memo: Where to Reallocate Budget
& Why

To · Performance Marketing Lead
From · Growth Analyst
Date · June 2026
Re · TWT Media Mix Optimisation

The Problem

50% of TWT's budget currently flows to Meta Ads — the lowest-ROAS channel at 2.1×. Google (3.4×) and Influencer (3.8×) are consistently outperforming but remain underfunded. The blended CAC of ₹418 is dragged up primarily by Meta's ₹520 CAC, which is 85% higher than Influencer's ₹280.


Funnel Gaps Identified

  • Cart abandonment is the biggest leak — 68% drop from Add-to-Cart to Purchase. Industry benchmark for D2C food is ~55%. TWT has a 13pt gap worth fixing before scaling spend.
  • Awareness-to-Click is weak on Meta — 2.1% CTR suggests creatives aren't landing. TWT's transparency-first messaging needs more education-format content (carousels, ingredient explainers) vs. direct conversion ads.
  • Repeat purchase rate at 28% is below potential — email/WhatsApp retention flows are likely underutilised given a health-conscious, loyal customer base.

Recommended Reallocation

↑ Google: 20% → 35%
TWT's customers are search-intent buyers — "high protein bar India", "clean label snacks". Google Search captures bottom-of-funnel demand at a 3.4× ROAS. Every ₹1 moved from Meta to Google here saves ~₹200 in CAC.
↑ Influencer: 30% → 40%
TWT's best-fit channel — nutritionist, fitness, and honest-review creators align perfectly with the brand's "nothing to hide" positioning. Highest ROAS (3.8×), lowest CAC (₹280). Micro-influencers (50K–300K) over mega.
↓ Meta: 50% → 25%
Don't cut Meta entirely — retargeting and lookalike audiences still work. But shift Meta's role from acquisition to retargeting cart abandoners and warming influencer audiences. Expected CAC drop of ₹60–80 blended.
